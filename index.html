What Neighbourhood is for

One local hub.
Three ways to make it useful.

Neighbourhood Pune is designed to help local commerce grow by making it easier for residents to discover independent businesses, for new founders to find their next step, and for shop owners to share what they offer.

01 / FOR SHOPPERS

Find your next local favourite.

Search Pune businesses by name, category, or neighbourhood. Browse a product catalogue and contact a seller to ask about current prices, availability, or how to order.

Explore the directory ↗
02 / FOR FOUNDERS

Turn an idea into a next step.

Explore practical starting points, find relevant local connections, and discover workshops or community events that can help you learn and keep moving.

Explore founder support ↗
03 / FOR SHOP OWNERS

Show people what you make.

Share a business profile and product catalogue, then use the inventory demo to keep a simple count of products on this device. Invite customers to contact you directly.

See the inventory demo ↗
What this site does today

It is a discovery, information, and community hub. The current version can filter sample listings, display product examples, track demo stock in one browser, and answer common questions with preset replies.

What it does not do yet

It does not take payments, place orders, sync stock between devices, verify listings, or send form submissions to a database. Those features need real business data and connected services.
